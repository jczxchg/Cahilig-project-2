Bachelor of Science in Criminology

The Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BS Crim) program provides a comprehensive foundation in the scientific study of crime, criminal behavior, law enforcement, and the administration of justice. Combining legal principles with tactical and forensic laboratory training, this degree equips students with the analytical, investigative, and ethical skills required to uphold public safety and order.

Students gain hands-on experience in crime scene investigation, forensic science, defensive tactics, and supervised on-the-job training (OJT) with law enforcement and correctional agencies. Graduates are prepared for the Criminologist Licensure Examination (CLE) and careers in the Philippine National Police (PNP), Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP), NBI, and forensic laboratories.

What You'll Learn

  • Criminological theories, crime causation, and human behavior
  • Philippine criminal law, jurisprudence, and criminal procedure
  • Criminal investigation, intelligence, and crime detection techniques
  • Forensic science, ballistics, dactyloscopy, and questioned documents
  • Law enforcement organization, patrol operations, and traffic management
  • Correctional administration, penology, and rehabilitation programs
  • Defensive tactics, marksmanship, and emergency response training
  • Criminological research, professional ethics, and field practicum (OJT)

Law Enforcement Administration & Public Safety

Maintaining peace and order, patrol operations, and community-oriented policing strategies.

Students gain tactical training, leadership skills, and operational knowledge required for modern policing. Passing the Criminologist Licensure Examination (CLE) qualifies graduates to join the Philippine National Police (PNP) as commissioned or non-commissioned officers, as well as the Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) and PDEA.

Forensic Science & Crime Laboratory Examination

Applying scientific methods in ballistics, dactyloscopy, questioned documents, and forensic chemistry.

Learn how to collect, preserve, and scientifically analyze physical evidence from crime scenes. Graduates are equipped to serve as forensic examiners, fingerprint technicians, and crime laboratory specialists in government agencies and private investigative firms.

Criminal Investigation & Intelligence Operations

Mastering crime detection, cybercrime investigation, surveillance, and intelligence gathering.

Develop analytical and investigative expertise to solve complex criminal cases and gather admissible court evidence. This pathway prepares graduates for investigative roles in the National Bureau of Investigation (NBI), PNP Criminal Investigation and Detection Group (CIDG), and anti-fraud units.

Fire Protection & Correctional Administration

Leading fire suppression, disaster response, penology, and offender rehabilitation programs.

Explore fire technology, arson investigation, institutional corrections, and community-based rehabilitation. Registered Criminologists are qualified to enter the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP), and Bureau of Corrections (BuCor).

Corporate Security Management & Pre-Law Track

Building a strong legal and risk-management foundation for corporate safety and Juris Doctor studies.

With extensive coursework in criminal law, evidence, and jurisprudence, BS Criminology serves as a strong pre-law degree for aspiring lawyers and prosecutors. Graduates also thrive as corporate security managers, industrial safety officers, and risk assessors in private enterprises.
