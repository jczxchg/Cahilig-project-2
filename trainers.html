Our Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Our Vision

KCC, as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.
