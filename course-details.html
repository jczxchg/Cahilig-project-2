Bachelor of Science in Psychology

The Bachelor of Science in Psychology program provides a strong scientific foundation in the study of human behavior, mental processes, and emotional development. Combining theoretical knowledge with empirical research and laboratory training, this degree equips students with analytical, interpersonal, and diagnostic skills applicable across clinical, educational, and corporate settings.

Students gain hands-on experience through psychological testing, experimental research, and supervised field practicums in real-world environments. Graduates are well-prepared for careers in human resources, mental health support, and research, as well as further graduate studies, medical school, or licensure as a Registered Psychometrician (RPm).

What You'll Learn

  • Core psychological theories and human development across the lifespan
  • Research methods, experimental design, and psychological statistics
  • Biological psychology, neuroanatomy, and cognitive processess
  • Counseling principles, basic interviewing, and intervention techniques
  • Industrial-organizational psychology, HR practices, and workplace behavior
  • Ethical standards, professional practice, and supervised field practicum
  • Abnormal psychology, psychopathology, and clinical diagnosis basics
  • Psychological assessment, test construction, and psychometric administration

Clinical Psychology & Mental Health Support

Understanding psychopathology, emotional resilience, and evidence-based therapeutic interventions.

Students gain foundational skills in diagnosing mental health conditions, conducting clinical interviews, and assisting in therapeutic programs. This track prepares graduates to work in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and community mental health clinics, or pursue a master's degree to become a licensed Clinical Psychologist.

Industrial-Organizational Psychology & HR

Applying psychological principles to workplace behavior, talent acquisition, and organizational growth.

Learn how to design employee training programs, evaluate workplace morale, and manage recruitment and organizational development. Graduates are highly sought after in corporate Human Resources departments, BPO industries, and management consulting firms across the Philippines and abroad.

Psychological Testing & Licensure (RPm)

Mastering test administration, scoring, interpretation, and ethical psychometric evaluation.

Our curriculum meets all PRC Board Licensure Examination requirements for Registered Psychometricians (RPm). Students receive hands-on laboratory training in administering standardized intelligence, personality, and aptitude tests used in corporate screening, clinical evaluation, and educational placement.

Educational Psychology & Guidance Services

Supporting student learning, developmental milestones, and school-based counseling programs.

Explore how cognitive and emotional factors influence academic success across childhood and adolescence. Graduates can serve as guidance associates, behavioral therapists for children with special needs, or pursue graduate licensure as a Registered Guidance Counselor (RGC).

Medical School Preparation & Behavioral Research

Building a strong scientific background in neurobiology, experimental design, and human physiology.

As a Bachelor of Science program, BSP includes natural science and laboratory courses that serve as an ideal pre-med foundation for aspiring physicians and psychiatrists. Students also complete an undergraduate thesis, equipping them for roles in academic, social, and market research.
